## About this guide

This guide provides general information about the fictional Contoso Assurance personal motor claim process. It does not contain customer or claim records. It does not change any policy wording and must not be used to decide coverage, liability, approval, denial, settlement, repair authorization, or payment.

For questions about an individual claim, the customer must complete verification and the agent must retrieve the record through the approved claim-status action.

## What Voice360 can help with

Voice360 can:

- Explain the general motor claim process.
- Explain common claim statuses.
- Describe commonly requested supporting documents.
- Retrieve the recorded status of an existing claim after customer verification.
- Explain the recorded missing documents and next action.
- Request a callback or human assistance.

Voice360 cannot:

- Open a new claim.
- Decide whether an incident is covered.
- Decide fault or liability.
- Approve or deny a claim.
- Negotiate or promise a settlement.
- Approve a repairer or repair estimate.
- Change bank or payment details.
- Give legal, medical, financial, or repair advice.

## Claim lifecycle

### Submitted

The claim has been received and assigned a claim number. Initial validation has not yet finished. The customer should keep the claim number for future contact.

### Information required

The claims team needs one or more documents or clarifications before assessment can continue. The recorded claim status should list the missing item and next step. Sending a document does not mean that the claim is approved.

### Under review

The claims team is reviewing the incident information, policy details, documents, and any available assessment. The claim remains undecided unless the claim record explicitly shows an approved or denied status.

### Assessment scheduled

A vehicle assessment or inspection has been arranged. The customer should follow the appointment details provided by the claims team. Rescheduling requires human assistance in this prototype.

### Approved

The claim record shows that the applicable review has been approved. Approval does not by itself confirm that payment has been sent or that repairs are complete. The recorded next action explains what follows.

### Payment processing

An approved payment is being prepared. Voice360 may repeat an expected payment date only when that date is returned by the claim-status action. Banking changes require a human representative.

### Paid

The claim record shows that payment was issued. The customer should allow normal banking processing time. A missing or disputed payment requires human assistance.

### Closed

The claims team has completed its current activity on the claim. Questions about reopening or disputing a closed claim require a human representative.

### Denied

The recorded decision is that the claim was not approved. Voice360 may explain only the reason text explicitly returned by the claim-status action. Questions, complaints, or challenges must be transferred to a human representative.

## Commonly requested information and documents

The required items depend on the incident and policy. The claim record is the source of truth for an individual customer.

### For most motor claims

- Completed incident description.
- Date, time, and location of the incident.
- Vehicle registration details.
- Driver and policyholder details.
- Clear photographs of visible damage, when safe to obtain.

### Collision involving another vehicle

- Other driver's name and contact information.
- Other vehicle's registration number.
- Other insurer's details, when available.
- Witness information, when available.
- Police or incident reference when one was created.

### Theft or attempted theft

- Police report or crime reference.
- List of stolen or damaged items connected with the insured vehicle.
- All available vehicle keys, when requested by the claims team.

### Repair assessment

- Vehicle photographs.
- Inspection details.
- Repair estimate when requested by the assigned claims team.

Do not send original documents unless a claims representative explicitly requests them. Do not include passwords, payment-card numbers, or unnecessary identity documents.

## Document quality guidance

- Upload or send a complete document rather than a cropped fragment.
- Make sure names, dates, and reference numbers are readable.
- Use the claim number in the approved upload or communication channel.
- Do not send documents through an unverified link or to an address that is not listed in the customer's official claim communication.
- If a document was already submitted but the claim still lists it as missing, request a human review. Processing may not be immediate.

## General timing guidance

Every claim is different. Voice360 must not promise a universal completion date.

- A newly submitted claim enters initial validation before assessment.
- A request for more information pauses or delays assessment until the required item is received and processed.
- Complex incidents, multiple vehicles, injuries, fraud concerns, disputes, and external assessments can take longer.
- The expected update date from an individual claim record is an estimate, not a guarantee.
- If the expected update date has passed with no new recorded update, the customer may request a callback.

## Claim communications

Customers should keep their contact details current through approved service channels. Voice360 cannot change contact, payment, or bank information in this prototype.

A genuine claims communication should identify the insurer and claim reference. A customer should not share passwords, one-time codes, complete card numbers, or complete bank credentials with Voice360 or an unsolicited caller.

If a customer suspects a fraudulent message or call, Voice360 should stop the standard journey and escalate the concern to a human representative.

## Privacy and verification

Voice360 must verify a customer before revealing claim-specific information. The capstone demonstration uses policy number, date of birth, and the last four digits of the registered phone number.

If verification fails, Voice360 cannot say which item was incorrect and cannot confirm whether a policy or claim exists. After two failed attempts, the customer must be offered human assistance.

## Human assistance

A customer can ask for a person at any time. Human assistance is also required for:

- Failed verification after two attempts.
- Suspected fraud or identity misuse.
- Complaints or disputed decisions.
- Legal action or legal representation.
- Serious injury or immediate danger.
- Bereavement, vulnerability, or financial hardship.
- Changes to payment, bank, personal, or policy details.
- Claim creation, reopening, withdrawal, or modification.
- A missing payment.
- Information that is not available from approved knowledge or claim data.

For immediate danger or urgent medical needs, the customer should contact local emergency services before continuing with the insurance process.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you check my claim?

Yes. Voice360 can retrieve an existing claim after verifying the customer using the approved verification process.

### Why do I need to verify myself?

Claim information is private. Verification helps prevent disclosure to someone who is not authorized to access it.

### Can you tell me which verification answer was wrong?

No. To protect the account, Voice360 gives the same general failure message regardless of which value did not match.

### Does “under review” mean my claim will be approved?

No. It means assessment is in progress. It is not a promise or prediction of a decision.

### Can you approve my repairs?

No. Repair authorization must come from the assigned claims team through an approved process.

### Can you change my bank details?

No. Payment or banking changes require secure handling by a human representative.

### I already sent a document. Why is it still missing?

The item may not have been processed or matched to the claim yet. If the record still lists it as missing, Voice360 can help request a human review.

### When will my claim be completed?

There is no universal completion time. Voice360 can repeat an expected update date only when one is present in the individual claim record. The date is an estimate, not a guarantee.

### Can I speak to a person?

Yes. A customer may request human assistance at any time.

### Can Voice360 file a new claim?

Not in this prototype. A human representative must help with a new claim.

### What if someone is injured?

If someone needs urgent medical attention or is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services first. Insurance servicing can continue after everyone is safe.
