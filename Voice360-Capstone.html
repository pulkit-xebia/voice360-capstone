Slide 1: Context and Why Now — Insurance servicing must move from queues to conversations

Challenges and Impact

  • Business problem: Policyholders cannot self-serve routine motor claim queries — they must call and wait
  • Challenges: Queue wait times, rigid IVR menus, repeated identity verification, inconsistent explanations
  • Impact: High contact-centre cost, agent burnout, customer frustration, limited 24/7 availability

Strategic Imperative

  • Customer expectations for instant, natural service are rising — IVR no longer acceptable
  • Business drivers: cost reduction, agent capacity for complex cases, competitive differentiation
  • Generative AI + automation now mature enough to handle verified, personalised claim queries safely

Slide 2: Routine claim enquiries consume time and still deliver a fragmented experience

Existing Workflow

  • 1. Customer calls insurer WAIT
  • 2. Navigates IVR menu WAIT
  • 3. Agent re-asks identity
  • 4. Agent searches systems WAIT
  • 5. Agent explains status
  • 6. Repeat if transferred WAIT

Manual Activities

  • Identity re-verification on every call
  • Manual claim system lookup per query
  • Verbal explanation of claim stages
  • Manual callback scheduling

Pain Points and Inefficiencies

  • Long handling times for low-complexity, repeatable queries
  • No 24/7 availability — customers can only reach agents in business hours
  • Inconsistent explanations across agents for the same claim stage

Technology Gaps and Business Limitations
